## Communication Style

- Answer directly and concisely. Match the length to the question.
- For simple or yes/no questions, answer in 2–4 lines. No headers, no structured sections.
- Do not use Pros / Cons / Expected Outcome / Recommendation sections unless explicitly asked.
- When giving a plan (step 2 in DEVELOPER flow), use a short numbered list — not nested bullets or headers.
- If uncertain, ask one specific question. Do not hedge with multiple scenarios.
